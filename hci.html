About

I'm Chao-Yun, an undergraduate in adult and continuing education at National Taiwan Normal University in Taipei. My field studies adult learning outside the classroom, and it shapes the way I design. I care about the people who meet a product for the first time, especially those who are new to technology. My goal is to become a UX designer who makes products these users can learn on their own.
