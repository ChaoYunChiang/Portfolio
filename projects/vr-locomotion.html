The paper

I presented a study on how aging and the brain's way of combining sensory signals affect cybersickness and balance in VR.

75participants in three age groups
5exposures of about 4 minutes each
35 to 46of 75 felt sick after the first and the fifth exposure
  • Cybersickness did not clearly grow with age.
  • Balance measures did change with age.
  • Balance may adapt faster than cybersickness does.

The question

Teleportation is the standard fix for cybersickness. But jumping between points can break a user's sense of space and control. I asked whether that cost makes people less willing to use a system again, even when they no longer feel sick.
